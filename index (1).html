Our Story

Born in Soshanguve.
Built with Pride.

Sosh Ink was born out of a deep passion for art and self-expression right here in Soshanguve, Block L. Since 2016, we've grown from a neighbourhood studio to one of Pretoria's most trusted tattoo and piercing destinations.

Our artists are more than technicians — they're storytellers. Every piece we create carries meaning, identity, and intention. We take the time to understand your vision, collaborate on the design, and execute with precision that speaks for itself.

We operate a strictly sterile studio. All needles are single-use, all surfaces are disinfected between clients, and our team holds certifications in skin safety and aftercare. Your health is never compromised for speed.
